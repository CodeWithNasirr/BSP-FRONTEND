# Daily Volume Increase Guide

**When to raise your Daily volume target — a simple, safe playbook for operators.**

This guide covers **two situations only**:

- **Scenario A — grow from 2,000 → 10,000.** Goal: **1,000 quality conversations in 7 days.**
- **Scenario B — grow from 10,000 → 100,000.** Goal: **5,000 quality conversations in 15 days.**

You don't need any technical knowledge. Each day you look at a few numbers on the dashboard and follow the rules below to **increase**, **hold**, or **decrease/pause** your Daily volume target.

---

## First, the 4 words you need to know

| Word | What it means (plain) | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily volume target** | How many messages you allow to go out today. **You set this.** | Automation → Control Panel |
| **Success rate** | Of the messages sent, how many were **delivered** (reached the phone). | Automation Dashboard |
| **Opt-out rate** | How many people replied STOP / unsubscribed. | Automation Dashboard |
| **Quality conversations (X of Y)** | Real replies counted toward your goal (the "1,000" or "5,000"). | Growth Plan page |

> **One golden rule for both scenarios: increase slowly, decrease quickly.** When in doubt, hold for one more day.

---

## The signals to watch (same for both scenarios)

Check these once a day before you change anything.

### Success rate (delivered)
```
[ ] 90% or higher  → GREEN  (healthy — safe to grow)
[ ] 80% – 89%      → AMBER  (hold, watch closely)
[ ] Below 80%      → RED    (decrease)
[ ] Below 70%      → the system will auto-pause on its own
```

### Opt-out rate (STOP / unsubscribe)
```
[ ] Under 1%       → GREEN  (safe to grow)
[ ] 1% – 3%        → AMBER  (hold or decrease)
[ ] 3% or higher   → RED    (the system will auto-pause on its own)
```

### Failures / errors
```
[ ] Under 5%       → GREEN
[ ] 5% – 15%       → AMBER  (check your template / list)
[ ] 15% or higher  → RED    (the system will auto-pause on its own)
```

> **The system already protects you.** It never sends more than your current limit, it warms up for the first few days on its own, and it **auto-pauses** if opt-outs, failures, or delivery cross the danger lines above. Your job is to grow volume *while the signals stay green*.

---

## The universal decision (read this once, use it every day)

| If today looks like… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Success **GREEN** + opt-out **GREEN** + you're **on or behind** your quality pace | **INCREASE** (small step) |
| Success GREEN + opt-out GREEN but you're **ahead** of pace | **HOLD** (no need to push) |
| Success **AMBER** or opt-out **AMBER** | **HOLD** one more day and watch |
| Success **RED**, or opt-out climbing toward 3%, or failures rising | **DECREASE** |
| System **auto-paused**, or opt-outs ≥ 3% | **PAUSE**, fix the cause, resume when green |

"Small step" = raise the Daily volume target by about **20–30%** (or by the fixed amount shown in each scenario). Never double it.

# Scenario A — 2,000 → 10,000

**Goal: 1,000 quality conversations in 7 days. Your ceiling is 2,000/day, so volume is never the limit — quality is.**

### 1. Starting Daily volume (Day 1)
```
[ ] Set Daily volume target = 500
[ ] Turn ON "Warm up gradually"
[ ] The system starts LOW on Day 1 (~150) and ramps to your 500 target by about Day 4 — automatically
```
You set **500**; the warm-up handles the gentle start for you. You do **not** need to hand-enter the small early numbers.

### 2. Day-by-day recommended volume range

| Day | The system aims for (with warm-up ON) | You may raise the target to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~150 | 500 (leave it) |
| 2 | ~225 | 500 |
| 3 | ~335 | 500 – 600 |
| 4 | ~500 (full target) | 500 – 700 |
| 5 | ~500–700 | up to 800 |
| 6 | ~600–800 | up to 900 |
| 7 | ~700–900 | up to 1,000 |

> All of these are far below your 2,000 ceiling, so you have room. Only climb the "raise to" column **if the signals stayed green** the day before.

### 3. When to INCREASE (all must be true)
```
[ ] Success rate is 90%+ (GREEN)
[ ] Opt-out rate is under 1% (GREEN)
[ ] No auto-pause happened yesterday
[ ] You are ON or BEHIND the pace table below
→ Raise the Daily volume target by ~100 (about +20–30%)
```

### 4. When to HOLD
```
[ ] Signals are only AMBER (success 80–89%, or opt-out 1–3%), OR
[ ] You are AHEAD of the pace table (no need to push harder)
→ Keep the same Daily volume target for one more day
```

### 5. When to DECREASE or PAUSE
```
DECREASE (lower the target by ~100–150) if:
[ ] Success rate drops below 80%, OR
[ ] Opt-out rate climbs above 1% and keeps rising, OR
[ ] Failures climb above 5%

PAUSE (stop, fix, resume when green) if:
[ ] The system auto-paused, OR
[ ] Opt-out rate hits 3%+, OR
[ ] Failures hit 15%+
Then check your template and contact list before resuming.
```

### 6. Success / opt-out signals to watch
- **Delivered (success):** keep it **90%+**. Below 80% = slow down.
- **Opt-out:** keep it **under 1%**. Above 3% = the system pauses you.
- **Replies:** more replies = faster progress toward the 1,000 goal. A template that gets **few replies** is your real bottleneck — change the template, don't just push volume.

### 7. How this ties to your Progression Plan (X of 1,000)
Open the Growth Plan and read **"X of 1,000 quality conversations."** Every real reply from your campaign is counted here automatically. Compare X to this pace:

| By end of… | You should be near |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | ~110 |
| Day 2 | ~250 |
| Day 3 | ~400 |
| Day 4 | ~560 |
| Day 5 | ~710 |
| Day 6 | ~860 |
| Day 7 | **1,000** |

```
[ ] BEHIND this line + signals green → INCREASE tomorrow
[ ] ON this line                     → HOLD
[ ] AHEAD of this line               → HOLD (bank the good quality)
[ ] BEHIND + signals amber/red       → do NOT push volume; fix quality/template first
```

# Scenario B — 10,000 → 100,000

**Goal: 5,000 quality conversations in 15 days. Your ceiling is 10,000/day. Bigger jump = go slower and use your best contacts first.**

> **Important for the big jump:** don't pour your whole list in. Message your **best contacts first** (recent buyers, repliers, engaged people), then work outward. Quality matters more here than ever.

### 1. Starting Daily volume (Day 1)
```
[ ] Set Daily volume target = 1,200
[ ] Turn ON "Warm up gradually"
[ ] The system starts LOW on Day 1 (~360) and ramps to your 1,200 target by about Day 4 — automatically
```

### 2. Day-by-day recommended volume range

| Day | The system aims for (warm-up ON) | You may raise the target to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~360 | 1,200 (leave it) |
| 2 | ~540 | 1,200 |
| 3 | ~800 | 1,200 |
| 4 | ~1,200 (full target) | 1,200 – 1,400 |
| 5–6 | ~1,200–1,400 | up to 1,600 |
| 7–9 | ~1,400–1,700 | up to 2,000 |
| 10–12 | ~1,700–2,000 | up to 2,500 |
| 13–15 | ~2,000–2,500 | up to 3,000 |

> Even 3,000/day is well under your 10,000 ceiling. Climb the "raise to" column **only while signals stay green**, and only as fast as your **best-contact pool** lasts.

### 3. When to INCREASE (all must be true)
```
[ ] Success rate is 90%+ (GREEN)
[ ] Opt-out rate is under 1% (GREEN)
[ ] No auto-pause happened yesterday
[ ] You still have good-quality contacts left to send to
[ ] You are ON or BEHIND the pace table below
→ Raise the Daily volume target by ~200–300 (about +20%)
```

### 4. When to HOLD
```
[ ] Signals are only AMBER (success 80–89%, or opt-out 1–3%), OR
[ ] You are AHEAD of the pace table, OR
[ ] You are running low on high-quality contacts (don't reach into cold contacts to hit a number)
→ Keep the same Daily volume target for one more day
```

### 5. When to DECREASE or PAUSE
```
DECREASE (lower the target by ~300–500) if:
[ ] Success rate drops below 80%, OR
[ ] Opt-out rate rises above 1% and keeps climbing, OR
[ ] Failures climb above 5%, OR
[ ] Reply quality falls as you move into colder contacts

PAUSE (stop, fix, resume when green) if:
[ ] The system auto-paused, OR
[ ] Opt-out rate hits 3%+, OR
[ ] Failures hit 15%+
```

### 6. Success / opt-out signals to watch
- **Delivered (success):** keep it **90%+**. This is the #1 signal at large scale.
- **Opt-out:** keep it **under 1%**. At 100k-scale ambitions, a rising opt-out rate is the fastest way to lose everything — respect it.
- **Watch the trend, not one day.** Two days of slowly rising opt-outs = decrease, even if you're not at 3% yet.
- **Template/segment:** if replies drop when you widen the audience, you've reached your quality edge — hold or narrow back to better contacts.

### 7. How this ties to your Progression Plan (X of 5,000)
Open the Growth Plan and read **"X of 5,000 quality conversations."** Compare X to this pace:

| By end of… | You should be near |
|---|---|
| Day 3 | ~600 |
| Day 6 | ~1,600 |
| Day 9 | ~2,700 |
| Day 12 | ~3,900 |
| Day 15 | **5,000** |

```
[ ] BEHIND this line + signals green + good contacts left → INCREASE tomorrow
[ ] ON this line                                          → HOLD
[ ] AHEAD of this line                                    → HOLD (protect quality)
[ ] BEHIND + signals amber/red                            → fix quality first, do NOT push volume
```

## Daily 3-minute routine (both scenarios)

```
[ ] 1. Open the Automation Dashboard — read Success rate and Opt-out rate.
[ ] 2. Any auto-pause or alert? Read the reason and clear it.
[ ] 3. Open the Growth Plan — read "X of Y quality conversations".
[ ] 4. Compare X to the pace table for today.
[ ] 5. Use the decision table: INCREASE / HOLD / DECREASE / PAUSE.
[ ] 6. If increasing, take ONE small step only. Save. Done.
```

## Things to never do
```
[ ] ❌ Never double your Daily volume in one jump.
[ ] ❌ Never push volume when success rate is red or opt-outs are rising.
[ ] ❌ Never override an auto-pause without fixing the cause first.
[ ] ❌ Never dump cold/old contacts in just to hit a daily number.
[ ] ❌ Never keep a template that gets almost no replies — change it.
```

## Remember
The **quality conversations counter (X of Y)** is the real scoreboard — not how many you sent. Grow volume only while **Success stays 90%+** and **Opt-outs stay under 1%**. Increase slowly, decrease quickly, and let the auto-pause protect you.
